Social Hub privacy

Last updated October 31, 2026

The ASME Ohio State Social Hub helps chapter officers prepare event announcements and schedule posts on chapter social accounts.

Public campaign information

Campaign titles, event details, descriptions, locations, advertising details, flyers, schedules, officer notes, and publishing history are stored in the chapter's public GitHub repository and may appear on the Hub. Treat campaign fields and uploads as public. Do not enter private member information or account credentials.

Account authorization

Officers authorize access to the chapter's repository and publishing accounts. Publishing credentials are kept in GitHub Actions secrets and used by the publishing workflow. LinkedIn Page publishing, when enabled and authorized, uploads campaign flyers and submits campaign text to LinkedIn on the chapter's behalf. The Hub records public image identifiers, post identifiers, links, timestamps, and publishing status. It does not use LinkedIn access to collect private messages or member contact lists.

Your browser

The Hub stores appearance and channel preferences in browser storage. Repository tokens stay in memory unless an officer chooses browser persistence. Local review mode can store draft campaigns in the browser. Offline support caches the application and public campaign data; authenticated API responses and publishing credentials are excluded from the offline cache. Clear browser site data to remove locally stored information.

Service providers

GitHub hosts the Hub, public repository, and workflows. Enabled integrations send campaign content to Instagram, GroupMe, or LinkedIn. Those providers handle information under their own policies.

Retention and removal

The chapter retains campaign history for officer handoff. Removing a campaign from the Hub stops future publishing and hides it from the dashboard while retaining publishing receipts and repository history. It does not automatically remove already published posts. Account authorization can be revoked through the relevant provider; officers should also remove obsolete Actions secrets. Contact the chapter to request review or removal of public campaign information. Copies can remain in repository history, downloads, caches, or destination services.

Contact

Contact ASME at Ohio State through the chapter website with privacy questions or removal requests.
